How could you become an AI expert?

You can start exploring AI right now by asking questions, experimenting carefully, and sharing what you learn.

Start small

Try explaining how a recommendation works or comparing an AI answer with a trusted source. Maths and coding can help, but curiosity comes first.

Learn together

Make a project with friends and ask who it helps. Practice listening, explaining your ideas, and changing them when you find a problem.

Think about it

What small AI question could you investigate this week?
