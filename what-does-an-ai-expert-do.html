What does an AI expert do?

AI experts do more than write code. They ask questions, work with others, test ideas, and learn from mistakes.

Understand the problem

Before building a tool, an expert talks with the people who might use it. A clear problem helps a team choose whether AI is even needed.

Build, test, improve

Experts examine data, try a design, and check results for errors or unfairness. Feedback from users helps them improve or change course.
