AI can help change the world

AI can help people explore big challenges, but making a positive difference takes care, evidence, and teamwork.

Find useful patterns

Researchers may use AI to study large sets of observations, such as changes in the environment. The tool can help them find questions worth investigating.

Measure what matters

A promising idea should be tested with the people it affects. Consider its benefits, mistakes, resource use, and who might be left out.

Think about it

What problem in your community would you want a team to tackle?
