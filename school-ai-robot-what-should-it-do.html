A school AI robot

A school robot could help with routine tasks, but students and teachers should help set its rules.

Find a useful job

It might help locate books or remind people about schedules. Its job should be clear so everyone knows what to expect.

Protect the school community

It should respect privacy, avoid unfair treatment, and ask an adult for help when it is unsure. Students deserve to understand and challenge its decisions.

Jamal helps the school robot

Jamal met a school robot that could answer questions about library books. When a younger student asked for a book about a private problem, the robot began to read the question aloud. Jamal quickly asked the librarian for help.

The class suggested that the robot should keep personal questions private and invite an adult to help when needed. They learned that designing a school tool means thinking about safety, privacy, and how different students might feel. The robot could help, but it could not replace a trusted person.

A real-life example

A school helper could remind students when the library closes, but it should not share a student's private question with everyone nearby. Helpful tools need clear privacy rules.

Fun fact

A barcode is a pattern of lines or shapes that a scanner can read to identify an item, such as a library book.

Write a school robot rule

  1. Choose one job for a school robot.
  2. Write one rule that keeps students safe.
  3. Think of a moment when the robot might be unsure.
  4. Decide which trusted adult it should ask for help.

Think about it

What is one task you would give a school robot, and one you would not?
