Where do we see AI today?

AI can appear in places you already know, from captions on videos to suggestions in a search box.

Everyday helpers

Speech-to-text can turn spoken words into captions. Navigation apps can use predictions to suggest a route when traffic changes.

Look more closely

Not every clever feature is AI, and an AI feature is not always the best option. Ask what information it uses and whether you can correct it.
